# Code of conduct

Jev Reflex Autonomy Lab welcomes participants of all backgrounds and experience
levels. This code applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project spaces, as well as when representing the project publicly.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and open to constructive feedback.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the people behind them.
- Help newcomers and respect different perspectives and experiences.
- Respect people's privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexual harassment, and
publishing someone's private information without permission are not acceptable.
Neither are sustained disruption, intimidation, or retaliation against someone
who reports a concern.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to Mahmood Khordoo at
[m.khordoo@gmail.com](mailto:m.khordoo@gmail.com). Include relevant context and
links where possible. Reports will be handled discreetly; information will only
be shared as needed to investigate and respond.

The maintainer may remove or edit inappropriate contributions and issue warnings,
temporary restrictions, or permanent bans depending on the severity and pattern
of behavior. Participants asked to stop unacceptable behavior must comply.
Requests to reconsider an enforcement decision can be sent to the same address.
